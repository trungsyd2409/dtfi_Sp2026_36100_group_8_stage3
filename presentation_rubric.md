Dear Students,

As part of your final project submission, you are required to prepare a fully recorded video presentation covering both Stage 1 and Stage 2 of your project.

Submission Requirements
1. PowerPoint Slides

Prepare slides that clearly summarise your project, including the problem definition, objectives, methodology, results, key findings, and progress achieved across Stage 1 and Stage 2.

2. Recorded Video Presentation

The total presentation duration must be 8–10 minutes, regardless of the number of group members.

Every group member must actively participate in preparing and delivering the presentation.

All group members must appear and speak in the recorded video, clearly explaining their respective contributions.

The presentation must be fully recorded in advance. No live presentation is required.

You may use Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or another suitable recording tool.

Ensure that the slides are clearly visible and that all speakers can be heard clearly.

Assessment – Individual Contribution and Understanding (3 Marks)
As there will be no live Q&A session, the original Q&A criterion (3 marks) will be replaced with Individual Contribution and Understanding (3 marks).

This criterion will assess:

Each member's active participation in the recorded presentation.

The clarity of each member's explanation of their individual contributions.

Each member's demonstrated understanding of the project's methodology, results, and key findings.

Marking guidelines:

3 marks: All members participate meaningfully, clearly explain their contributions, and demonstrate strong understanding.

2 marks: Participation is evident, but explanations or understanding lack depth.

1 mark: Limited participation or unclear explanations of individual contributions.

0 marks: No meaningful evidence of individual participation or understanding.

Submission via Canvas
You must submit both the PowerPoint slides and the fully recorded video presentation through Canvas by the specified deadline.

Please ensure that your presentation is professional, well-organised, and rehearsed before submission.
